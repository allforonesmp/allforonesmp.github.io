First Steps

  1. Read and agree to the server rules above.
  2. Download and move AutoModpack and Defixus Anti-Cheat into your mods folder
  3. Put the server I.P adress into the adress bar in java edition: 203.7.6.56
  4. Paste in the AutoModpack fingerprint located under Access the server I.P and click verify
  5. AutoModpack will download all of the mods to your client and then you restart minecraft
  6. After joining you need to register your account, type /register PASSWORD PASWORD E.G: /register soup40 soup40 That will set your password to soup40.
  7. You may also be prompted apon joining with something saying /login, this means you type /login PASSWORD E.G: /login soup40 This will log you into your account. This only tells you to do it if you have not logged in within 5 hours or if you have switched computers or wifi networks
